    How the detector works.

    Each item passes through intake, a modality-specific analysis, score fusion and a report. No single signal decides the verdict; a strong indicator alone yields Medium, several together yield High.

    Email and text

    TF-IDF (word and character n-grams) logistic-regression classifier; URL look-alike, punycode, shortener and link-mismatch checks; SPF/DKIM/DMARC, Reply-To and display-name spoofing checks; urgency, credential, payment and secrecy cues; stylometric estimate of machine-written text.

    Images

    EXIF and tool metadata, error-level analysis, FFT spectrum analysis for upsampling artefacts, sensor-noise consistency, QR-code decoding and brand-logo reuse (ORB feature matching against logos/).

    Audio

    Decoded with ffmpeg to 16 kHz mono; band-limit and fricative energy, noise-floor, pitch variation, spectral-flux regularity and metadata checks for cloned or synthetic voices.

    Video

    Uniform frame sampling, face detection, face-versus-surroundings sharpness, temporal flicker and jitter, plus the full audio analysis on the soundtrack.

    Limits you should know

    These are probabilistic checks built on classical models and signal statistics, not a substitute for human judgement. The bundled text corpus is a small seed set; retrain with a real dataset (python -m shieldscan.train_text --csv data.csv) before relying on it. Always confirm money or credential requests through a second channel.

    ShieldScan runs on this computer. Scans send nothing to third parties; the Inbox tab talks only to the mail server you choose.
